# U.S. Housing Market and Monetary Policy

## Research Question

How are changes in U.S. monetary policy associated with mortgage rates, housing prices, and existing home sales?

## Motivation

Housing markets are closely connected to interest rates, household borrowing costs, and broader economic conditions. This project examines the relationship between monetary policy and housing market dynamics in the United States.

## Data Sources

The project will use publicly available U.S. economic data, with sources including:

- **Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED):** https://fred.stlouisfed.org/
- **National Association of Realtors (NAR):** https://www.nar.realtor/

The final analysis will document the selected series, sample period, units, and data-processing procedures.

## Planned Methods

1. Collect and document relevant economic time series.
2. Clean and visualize the data using Python.
3. Examine trends in mortgage rates, housing prices, and home sales.
4. Conduct exploratory statistical analysis.
5. Discuss limitations and distinguish correlation from causation.
